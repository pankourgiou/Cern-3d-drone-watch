CERN · 3D DRONE WATCH

tempo 70 BPM (50–105)

Hover or tap a calculation: the drone flies to the part of CERN it belongs to, that part lights up, and the tempo follows (50–105 BPM). Tap again to lock. Switching is limited to one card per beat, never faster than 105 BPM: switch too fast and the light turns red until you slow down, then green again. Left alone, the run cycles forever: injection, ramp, stable beams, dump, recovery, then a new fill and a new search.
